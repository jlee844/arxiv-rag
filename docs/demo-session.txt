POPE (Polling-based Object Probing Evaluation) is a benchmark for evaluating object existence hallucinations in vision-language models [2]. It reveals that even state-of-the-art VLMs exhibit substantial false positive rates [2]. The POPE benchmark measures accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score across Random, Popular, and Adversarial splits (3,000 questions each) [5]. The Adversarial split is particularly critical as it targets objects frequently co-occurring but absent in the specific image [5].
